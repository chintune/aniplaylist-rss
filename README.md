## Music discovery, made simple

AniPlaylist brings seasonal anime music into one searchable catalog. Pick a season, find a song, then open it in your preferred music service or watch its video when one is available.

<table>
  <tr>
    <td width="50%" valign="top">
      <strong>Search & explore</strong><br>
      Search anime, song titles, or artists. Switch between English, Romaji, and Japanese title variants where available.
    </td>
    <td width="50%" valign="top">
      <strong>Filter your way</strong><br>
      Browse OP, ED, insert songs, OSTs, and other releases. Narrow the list to tracks with videos.
    </td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td width="50%" valign="top">
      <strong>Listen anywhere</strong><br>
      Open matching tracks on Spotify or Apple Music whenever a link is available.
    </td>
    <td width="50%" valign="top">
      <strong>Watch in-page</strong><br>
      Play supported AnimeThemes videos from the catalog without leaving the website.
    </td>
  </tr>
</table>

## Seasonal RSS feeds

Subscribe to a season in your favourite RSS reader to keep up with new matching releases. Feeds refresh automatically on a schedule (about every 30 minutes).

| Season | Feed |
| --- | --- |
| Fall 2026 | [Open RSS feed](https://chintu-io.github.io/aniplaylist-rss/rss/fall-2026.xml) |
| Winter 2027 | [Open RSS feed](https://chintu-io.github.io/aniplaylist-rss/rss/winter-2027.xml) |
| Spring 2027 | [Open RSS feed](https://chintu-io.github.io/aniplaylist-rss/rss/spring-2027.xml) |
| Summer 2027 | [Open RSS feed](https://chintu-io.github.io/aniplaylist-rss/rss/summer-2027.xml) |
| Fall 2027 | [Open RSS feed](https://chintu-io.github.io/aniplaylist-rss/rss/fall-2027.xml) |

RSS entries use a simple, readable format:

```text
[OP] Anime Title
Artist - Song Name
```

Each entry links back to its AniPlaylist release page. A season feed can be available even when the source hasn't published matching tracks for that season yet.
