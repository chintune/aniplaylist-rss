## Music discovery, made simple

AniPlaylist brings seasonal anime music into one searchable catalog. Pick a season, find a song, then open it in your preferred music service or watch its video when one is available.

| Feature | What you can do |
| --- | --- |
| Search & explore | Search anime, song titles, or artists in English, Romaji, and Japanese where available. |
| Filter | Browse OP, ED, insert songs, OSTs, and tracks with videos. |
| Listen | Open songs on Spotify or Apple Music when links are available. |
| Watch | Play supported AnimeThemes videos without leaving the site. |
